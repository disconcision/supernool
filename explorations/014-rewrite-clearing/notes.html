A place to reshape an expression.

Walk into the old tree’s circle. Its branches settle into a working plane, and clicking a rune selects the expression growing above it. The side panel offers the rewrites valid at that junction. The whole-tree list provides another way to select.

The starting tree has 13 nodes: ((2×x + 0) + y) + (3×x + 0). The challenge is to reveal 5×x + y, which needs only five. Moves operate on the actual expression, so you can take different routes, undo, and try again.

Playing

WASD or arrows move the avatar on the ground; clicking the ground walks toward that spot. Rocks block movement. “Walk to the tree” follows the entry path. Right-drag orbits, and the wheel zooms. Walking out of the circle lets the tree spread into space again.

“Suggest a next move” selects a junction and outlines a possible action. It does not perform the move. Undo and redo restore whole expression states. Reloading starts over; there is no saved game yet.

A six-move solution
Swap the root branches → regroup to the left → remove the first zero → remove the second zero → factor out x → combine 3 + 2.

The appearance palette

Open Appearance for face-aware studio/cel shading, smooth alternatives, hewn or rounded tips, thickness, taper, bow and its variation, height variation, twist, facet widths and junction blending. “Another shape seed” changes the deterministic irregularities. Resting spread applies outside the circle; interaction brings the tree back to its working plane.

Performance and motion

The two background trees retain static meshes. The active tree is rebuilt in a worker with trimmed geometry buffers transferred back to the scene. Pending poses are coalesced, so walking and camera input do not wait for field sampling. Tree animation still depends on how quickly the worker produces new meshes. Sampling 80 is the play default; 96 and 144 are available for inspection.

Stable node identities carry surviving parts between layouts. Swaps take a depth arc, removed leaves retract, and changed attachments follow parent paths. Factoring records which repeated branch merges into the retained one. This is an initial general motion policy: swollen junctions, intersecting branches and uneven intermediate shapes remain possible. It does not yet include all the specialized associativity alternatives preserved in studies 012 and 013.

What was checked

The six-move route was completed through the browser interface, including factoring and arithmetic. Direct rune selection, undo/redo, cel shading, a bow adjustment and walking out of the circle were checked. Automated checks covered 738 legal moves on reachable expressions, numeric equivalence samples, unique occurrence IDs on the solution route and finite motion samples. These are regression checks, not a proof of all geometry or a general algebra theorem prover.

Next questions

Does selecting a subtree feel natural? Does the tree make the scope of a rewrite legible? Does simplification feel like changing a place? This study concentrates on those questions. It uses a small standalone typed algebra implementation; integration with nool’s generalized rule engine, terrain roots, persistent surface decorations, freeform hand dragging and user-authored trees remain future work.
