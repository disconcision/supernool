## Scorch prompt

Production game texture extraction from attached scene reference. Create ONE square monochrome SCORCH COVERAGE MASK, seen from directly overhead with NO perspective. White background means empty/untouched ground; black means full char coverage, grays mean partial soot coverage. This is a flat black-on-white mask, NOT a scene, no transparency checkerboard.
Translate the approved scorch's visual idea into a usable top-down decal: dense irregular black cinder center, mostly SHORT straight outward blast striations of highly unequal lengths and uneven spacing, and just THREE exceptionally long thin tapering streaks extending continuously from the same center. They must remain connected to the central burn across their whole lengths, strongest at the center and weaker at the ends. Long streaks arranged asymmetrically, not evenly spaced. Most marks end within inner half of overall footprint. Flat instant explosive scorch, no roots, tentacles, winding cracks, satellite patches or regular sunburst. Restrained broad soot breakup with a little soft gray ash near the core; no excessive fine brush texture. Preserve an uninterrupted pure white margin at least 10% around entire asset, all tips safely inside. No tree, rocks, soil, grass, shadows, highlights, purple, runes, labels, frame, grid or other content. Grayscale mask only; ink-black scorch on solid pure white.

## Footing atlas prompt

Production game terrain mask atlas. Output ONE wide rectangular grayscale image with three distinct rock-footing coverage masks spaced in three equally sized SQUARE cells side by side, one horizontal row. Pure WHITE background, BLACK marks opaque, gray partial coverage. No transparency checkerboard, no dividers, labels or borders.
These are flat top-down ground patches placed beneath basalt rocks, to integrate them with muted mossy earth. No rocks themselves, no directional cast shadow. Each patch must have an irregular strong dark center that gradually thins into a few broad soft broken earth shapes. Cell 1: compact squat asymmetric oval, dusty soft edges. Cell 2: elongated uneven footprint, about twice as long as wide, compressed soil under a rock bank. Cell 3: broader angular broken-edge footprint, a few coarse lobes for sparse moss-soil transition, not circular wreath. Each silhouette has very little interior detail. Only low-frequency broad forms, 4-7 major edge variations. Avoid botanical detail, lichen rosettes, grass blades, pebbles, speckles, busy brushwork, tiny scratches or fibrous edges. Keep all cells separate with 15% pure white padding on every side of every square cell. Use solid black through dark gray in centers and smooth muted grays at perimeter, ending at pure white. No scene, perspective, lighting, color or text. This is a reusable mask sheet, not a painting of soil.
