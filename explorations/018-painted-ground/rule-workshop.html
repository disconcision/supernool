01 / A VISUAL VOCABULARY

Recognition before ornament.

Choose a rule to compare its four treatments. The formula stamp states the actual move; the seal is quicker to spot; the motion sketch starts to suggest what your hands do.

Identity versus available move

All the formulas here equate expressions with the same value. The arrow version marks an available rewrite direction; the bar version treats the two rows as an identity. The bar is a graphic separator, not division.

Swap and regroup work both ways. Factoring and expansion are separately equipped moves. Removing +0 or ×1 and combining numbers currently go only toward the simpler form. The identity itself is still symmetric; these tiles do not add the missing reverse interactions.

At the size of an inventory slot

Arrow and bar versions at 40, 56 and 80 pixels. Short formulas survive shrinking better; association is the stress test. Focus or hover any tile to read its full name and identity.
