Variation without starting over.

The hewn surface approach remains. This pass fixes ineffective controls, gives generated trees distinct branching planes, and reduces repeated rendering work.

What changed

  • Height variation is applied after operation layout, so it now affects main operands instead of being overwritten.
  • Spatial generated trees use different branch-plane orientations at successive depths. Flattening returns them to the working plane.
  • Bow arches relative to each branch. Seeded variation changes its local direction rather than biasing every branch toward the same world axis.
  • The ground slightly overlaps the trunk base. Roots following rock cracks are recorded for the world study; they are not implemented.
  • All four shading options remain. Geometry is reused for shading/label changes, and stationary views stop redrawing.

Where we are

Both face-aware studio and cel have a place; smoother trees also belong in the broader palette. Moderate twist, strong taper, local bow, restrained blend and height variation give enough variety to start testing actual encounters. Extreme slider settings remain experiments, not recommended encounter defaults.

I recommend moving to a small rocky clearing with an avatar, a few varied trees and one actively rewritten tree. Test approach, flattening, manipulation and return to spatial form. Cache inactive geometry. This should reveal more than another isolated shader round.

Still unresolved: clean ridges and junctions, collision-free unfolding, terrain-aware roots, persistent surface attachments, and the cost of many animated trees. This pass is not a final world renderer.

Checks

10 height-response cases, 252 motion/embedding states, 12 spatial generated-tree seeds, 24 extracted meshes, and the existing 1,818-state retraction checks passed. Browser checks covered height extrema, generated trees from the side, studio/cel shading, and geometry reuse.
