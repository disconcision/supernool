What this round established

Broad faces can be made much more legible without reintroducing the collapsing ports from 010. The geometry uses seven-sided sections and flat cuts at exposed ends. The face-aware normal calculation follows those sections per pixel, rather than averaging their borders across mesh triangles. Cel shading is available separately in the main study.

The spatial slider spreads branches through depth; returning it to zero restores the working embedding. It changes the presentation, not the term. This first version primarily changes depth coordinates—it is not a botanical growth model or a collision-free unfolding algorithm.

Comparison notes

  • Closer: broad light/dark faces, sharp longitudinal borders, cut tips, intact carried fork, and legible before/transfer/after states.
  • Still different: the concept has cleaner silhouettes and more deliberate face flow through the forks. The live midpoint still forms a broad elbow; some grid-scale waviness remains, especially at strong twist.
  • Concept caveat: the generated spatial row follows the connections, but reads flatter than intended. The slider and side camera are better evidence for actual depth.
  • Performance: 144 sampling improves outlines but costs more per frame. Select 96 for faster exploration. The displayed rebuild time excludes GPU shading cost.
  • Unresolved: collision avoidance, persistent UVs, decorations following surface changes, and clean feature-preserving junction meshing.
