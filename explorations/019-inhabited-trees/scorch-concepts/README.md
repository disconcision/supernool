## Prompt 1

Use case: precise-object-edit / stylized-concept.
Edit target: attached Supernool scene screenshot.
Create a scene-integrated concept of a dramatic LIGHTNING STRIKE SCORCH across the ground beneath the central tree.
Keep the same isometric camera, tree silhouette and location, ring of rocks, character, terrain palette and restrained stylized rendering. Remove UI text and corner buttons for the concept.
At the foot of the tree is a concentrated cinder-black impact area. From this center LONG irregular radiating electrical burn scars fork and subdivide across the earth like lightning discharge tracks / Lichtenberg branching. Several strongest burnt striations extend all the way to the surrounding rock circle and a few continue beyond through its gaps. Black charred tracks have broken soot edges, taper strongly, with some smaller subsidiary forks. Strong dramatic spatial reach, varied lengths and directions, asymmetric. Ground remains visible in large wedges between the tracks. The mark must look scorched INTO existing ground, flat, with no raised arms.
This is aftermath of a strike, NOT a lightning bolt currently hitting, NOT tentacles, roots, tar, a puddle, a blobby star-shaped organism, a crater or an explosion. No smooth curly lobes. No smoke, glow, purple, runes or magic circles in this first version. Broad readable burn marks rather than excessive tiny brushwork. Respect the ground perspective: scars follow the terrain and disappear behind the rocks. One full scene image, no panels.
