Radii follow r(parent)² = r(left)² + r(right)² here. This is a chosen design constraint, not a universal biological law.

What this tests

Explicit polygon rings, narrow bevel faces and a triangulated junction patch replace the distance field. With zero bow and twist, long side faces are planar. Bow and twist introduce a few deliberate longitudinal sections; they can still produce visible diagonal changes where a warped quad is triangulated.

The junction is a convex patch between three cut openings. It is deliberately exposed for criticism: it can look too geometric or pinched, and it is not yet the smoothly carved saddle in the image. The topology check reports open or multiply shared edges, not every possible self-intersection.

This isolates the material geometry before adding tree rewrites. The previous moving trees remain in 007, whose hewn field is not an accepted finish.
