A grip and a brace.

The hands are the manipulation interface. Hold a rune and drag toward an outlined destination. Pull back to reverse the change; release near the destination to complete it. An early release lets the structure settle back without changing the expression.

Try it

  1. Walk into the circle, or use “Walk to the tree.”
  2. Choose “Suggest a grip” to see a possible starting contact. The suggestion does not make the move.
  3. Hold the gold-marked rune, pull toward the outlined ring, and release there.
  4. Try a partial pull and return. Then try another route, using undo when needed.

Mouse dragging drives the held structure. G grabs/releases the selected rune; arrows move that grip. WASD walks Lehi and can contribute force through body movement during a keyboard grip. Space can also be held to grip. Tab exchanges which hand leads, and Escape cancels. Right-drag orbits and the wheel zooms when no grip is active.

What the two hands do

One hand grips the moving branch. The other braces a shared junction or receives a matching branch during factoring. It is automatically positioned in this study; the two hands are not independently controlled yet. They open at rest, curl around contacts, and return to Lehi after release.

The provisional character uses the short blue-green mantle direction from the other task’s avatar blockouts, with ordinary arms and separate enlarged tools. The tools have three fingers and an opposed thumb, following the current reference notes. This does not select a final character design.

A small sigil variation

Appearance includes inscribed stone sigils and the simple discs, alongside the retained hewn, cel, smooth and shape controls. This is a modest visual experiment. A fuller comparison with the reference lab’s tree symbols remains worthwhile.

How direct is it?

Your displacement controls a continuous pose along a valid rewrite path. You can stop and reverse before committing. This is constrained direct manipulation rather than freeform physics: the algebra supplies possible destinations, and the tree may arc through depth while the input guide is projected on screen. The mesh is produced in a worker and can trail very fast input.

The full challenge works through physical gestures: swap, regroup, press in two zeros, factor the matching x branches, then gather the numeric pair into its junction. The browser playthrough reached 5 nodes from 13 in six drags. A held partial pose, reversal, hand exchange and cancellation were checked separately. Junction finish, collision-free surfaces and independent second-hand placement remain open.
