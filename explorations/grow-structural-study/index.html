What these drawings establish
They establish discrete structure and a repeatable layout—not a continuous deformation. Intermediate scaffolds can have temporary arity or a forest of roots; only the endpoints are committed algebraic terms. Plateaus are a uniform family of nested solid shelves, not enclosing glass walls.
